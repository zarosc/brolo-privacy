Brolo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Brolo is a free app for planning, growing and looking after a vegetable garden.

In short: Brolo has no accounts, no server, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. What you enter stays on your phone. The developer does not receive your data.

1. Who is responsible

Brolo is published under the name ZAIOs Mobile. The data controller is Oscar Zamunaro, a private individual. For any question about this policy or your data: zaro.applications@gmail.com.

2. What data the app uses and where it is stored

To work, Brolo keeps in a database on your phone:

  • your garden: the zones you draw or measure by walking, with their coordinates, exclusions, obstacles, water source, soil and preferences;
  • the season plan, tasks, calendar and the varieties you add;
  • the diary: notes, harvests, problems and the photos you take;
  • your tools, with any photos and notes; if you write it, also the name of the person you lent a tool to.

This data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, except in the cases described in section 4.

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Location (precise or approximate, your choice, only while you use the app): used to move the map to your garden when you tap “My location”. The phone's location is not stored and not sent to anyone; the app only stores the zones you draw.
  • Camera: when you choose to take a photo for the diary or for a tool, and while measuring by walking.
  • Notifications: reminders are created by the phone itself, without any server.
  • Start at boot and vibration (granted at install): to schedule the reminders again after a restart and to make them vibrate.
  • Internet and network state: to download the aerial photos for the map.

You can deny or revoke any permission in your phone settings: the app keeps working, without the feature that needs it.

4. What leaves your phone, and to whom

  • Aerial photos (AGEA). The map uses orthophotos from AGEA, the Italian Agricultural Payments Agency (geoportale.agea.gov.it). To show them, the app requests from AGEA's service the map tiles you are looking at. As with any website, AGEA receives your IP address and which tiles are requested. Brolo does not send AGEA your location or any other data.
  • Phone location services. When you tap “My location”, Android works out your position; depending on your phone settings, it may use Google's location services.
  • Measuring by walking (augmented reality). This feature uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Android backup. If Google backup is turned on on your phone, Android may copy Brolo's database to your Google account, as it does for other apps; the diary and tool photos are not copied. The backup is managed by Google and you can turn it off in your phone settings.
  • When you share. If you use “Export” or share the shopping list, the file or text goes only where you choose. The exported file also contains your garden's location; the photos are not in it.

Climate data, soil data and the list of Italian municipalities are included in the app: using them sends no request.

5. What Brolo does not do

Brolo does not ask you to create an account, does not show ads, does not use analytics, tracking or profiling tools, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete all of it from Settings → Privacy and data → Delete data, or by uninstalling the app. From the same page you can also export a copy with “Export”. Copies in Google backup are managed from your Google account.

7. Your rights

Since the data is on your phone and the developer does not receive it, you can view, export and delete it directly in the app. For any question you can write to zaro.applications@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

8. Changes

If the app changes how it uses data, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. The history of changes is public on GitHub.

9. Contact
